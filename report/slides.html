The problem

A great product that’s hard to find

Lisha Hall makes prickly pear and mesquite bean jelly and cinnamon rolls from fruit hand-picked on our family’s ranch.

She sells them once a month at Goliad Market Days in Goliad, Texas.

She has always wanted a website she can point market customers to.

What it costs her today

  • Customers who want more between markets can’t find her
  • Every question (“spicy one in stock?”, “gift box?”, “next market?”) is a phone call
  • Website builders cost $17–$49/month, and she can’t use an online store: stock depends on the harvest

How the agent works

One small agent, fenced in by code

1. VisitorAsks in the chat box on Products or Market
→
2. Server functionLength checks, per-visitor and daily limits, answer cache
→
3. AgentSite facts + question → Portkey → gpt-5.6-luna
Tool: suggest_contact
→
4. Output guardsPrompt-leak canary, invented-price check, loop and length caps
→
5. AnswerPlus “Send this question to Lisha” when it doesn’t know

Hand-off, not guesswork

Unknown prices, hours, allergens, or custom orders become a pre-filled Contact message the visitor reviews and sends. It reaches Lisha’s inbox by email.

Nothing it can break

No secrets, no payments, no write access. The model key stays on the server, and the agent’s only action drafts a message.

How I kept it down

  • Small model, ~80-word answers
  • Facts in the prompt instead of tool lookups: 2.0 → 1.3 calls per question
  • Repeated opening questions answered from a cache: 0 tokens

Did it hurt quality? No

Same 8 graded questions, two runs: 8/8 for both designs. The cheaper one was 7–22% cheaper and ~2× faster.
